## 2024-11-20 - Surfacing Hidden Costs in Idle UIs
**Learning:** In idle/incremental games, task buttons often hide critical decision-making info (costs/yields). Forcing users to click or guess causes frustration and inefficient play. Using native UI tooltips is a highly effective, low-friction pattern to reveal this data on-demand without cluttering the UI.
**Action:** Always surface hidden requirements and yields for interactive tasks via tooltips if there isn't enough screen real estate to show them inline.
